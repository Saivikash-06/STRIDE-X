# STRIDE-X – DoSJE Platform

### Smart Tracking & Real-time Inspection for Development and Empowerment

**STRIDE-X** is a digital platform designed to connect citizens, government departments, NGOs, universities, and industries to collaboratively identify and solve societal challenges.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

**SIH 2026 – PS095**

Develop a digital platform to crowdsource societal challenges and facilitate collaborative problem-solving through universities and industry partnerships.

## ✨ Key Features

- 👥 Citizen problem reporting
- 🏛️ Government department collaboration
- 🤝 NGO and industry partnerships
- 🎓 University student solution showcase
- 📍 Location-based problem tracking
- 📊 Real-time problem status monitoring
- 💰 Budget allocation and expenditure tracking
- ⏱️ Expected and actual resolution time tracking
- 🔍 Transparent progress monitoring
- 📅 Government inspection history

## 🎯 Objectives

- Encourage citizens to report societal challenges.
- Connect problems with relevant organizations.
- Promote collaboration between government, NGOs, universities, and industries.
- Improve transparency in funding and project execution.
- Track problem resolution from submission to completion.
